Example interaction:
1. Click Add Policy on Health Secure Plus.
2. My Policies displays Health Secure Plus, type Health, premium ₹6,500, and Active (Demo).
3. Click Remove to remove it from the demo list.
4. Enter a name, policy number, and claim details in Submit a Claim Request.
5. A confirmation message appears: Demo claim submitted for [name] (policy [policy number]).
